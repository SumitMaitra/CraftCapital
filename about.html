About CraftCapital

Our Mission

CraftCapital is a verified B2B marketplace dedicated to connecting West Bengal's rich terracotta and clay-craft artisan clusters directly with institutional buyers. Our mission is to eliminate middlemen, ensure fair pricing for artisans, and provide a seamless, reliable procurement experience for businesses.

We are currently focused on empowering the master craftspeople of Panchmura and Krishnanagar.

🤝

The Problem We Solve

Institutional buyers (corporate gifting, hotels, retailers) want authentic crafts, but sourcing is fragmented. Existing channels fall short:

  • Amazon Karigar & B2C Platforms: Focused on single-unit retail, lacking B2B infrastructure like bulk negotiation, custom sampling, and proper GST invoicing.
  • Instagram / Direct Outreach: Highly unreliable communication, trust deficits, and zero logistics support for large orders.
  • Government Emporiums (e.g., Biswa Bangla): High markups, rigid catalog options, and lack of direct connection with the artisan.

Our Approach: We offer curated discovery, dedicated B2B procurement assistance, and industry insights through our Kolkata Craft Weekly digest.

The Team

Our Artisan Clusters

📍 Panchmura (Bankura)

Famous for the iconic Bankura Horse and robust terracotta artifacts. Panchmura crafts represent the rustic, earthy heritage of Bengal, characterized by their deep reddish hues and intricate details.

📍 Krishnanagar (Nadia)

Renowned for its incredibly lifelike clay dolls and miniature figurines. The artisans of Ghurni, Krishnanagar, possess an uncanny ability to capture human expressions and everyday scenes in clay.

Our Journey & Roadmap

Year 1: Kolkata Pilot

Onboarding the top 100 artisans from Panchmura and Krishnanagar. Partnering with 50 local institutional buyers in Kolkata to refine the procurement workflow.

Year 2: National Expansion

Expanding buyer reach across major Indian metros. Introducing 3 new artisan clusters from West Bengal, including Dokra and Sholapith crafts.

Year 3: Eastern India Hub

Scaling the platform to include heritage crafts from Odisha, Bihar, and Assam, establishing CraftCapital as the premier B2B hub for Eastern Indian artistry.
