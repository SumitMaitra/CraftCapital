About Panchmura

Located in the Bankura district, Panchmura is world-renowned for its distinctive long-necked terracotta horses. Originally crafted as ritualistic offerings to the Sun God (Dharam Thakur), these iconic pieces have become a symbol of Indian handicraft. The cluster is characterized by its earthy red firing techniques and deeply ingrained SHG structures.

🎭

About Krishnanagar

Located in the Nadia district, Krishnanagar boasts a 400-year-old tradition of remarkably lifelike clay doll making, patronized by Maharaja Krishnachandra. Unlike Panchmura's robust firing, Krishnanagar pieces often involve sun-baked clay built on wire armatures, known for exquisite detailing, realistic proportions, and vibrant hand-painting.
