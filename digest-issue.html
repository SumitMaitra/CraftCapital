Feature Story

Panchmura Horses: From Rural Hearths to Corporate Boardrooms

🐴

For centuries, the terracotta horses of Panchmura were crafted strictly for ritualistic purposes in rural Bengal. Placed under village trees as offerings to local deities, their distinctive long necks and pointed ears have always stood as a symbol of devotion.

But over the past decade, a quiet revolution has taken place. Today, these same iconic shapes are gracing the lobbies of five-star hotels in Tokyo, executive boardrooms in Mumbai, and corporate gifting hampers across Europe.

"We never imagined our ritual horses would sit on the desks of CEOs. We had to learn how to pack them for flights, and how to scale production without losing the soul of the craft." — Master Artisan Ranjit Das

The transition wasn't simple. Institutional buyers demand consistency, high volume, and robust packaging—concepts that were historically foreign to cluster production. At CraftCapital, our mission has been to bridge this exact gap. By verifying Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and implementing quality control protocols at the source, we ensure that corporate buyers receive authentic heritage pieces with modern B2B reliability.
