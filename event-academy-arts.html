'Mitti Ka Roop': 400 Years of Krishnanagar Clay Realism

A curated fine art retrospective displaying the hyper-realistic clay miniature tradition patronized by Maharaja Krishnachandra in the 18th century.

🗓️September 2 – September 8, 2026
📍Academy of Fine Arts (Cathedral Road), Kolkata
⏰3:00 PM – 8:00 PM Daily

The 400-Year Legacy of Ghurni

The potters of Ghurni on the banks of river Jalangi have long been celebrated for their astonishing anatomical precision and realism. Unlike religious idol sculptors, Krishnanagar doll makers capture everyday human moments: the local vegetable seller, the folk baul singer, the village weaver, and intricate pastoral life vignettes.

This exhibition, held at Kolkata's historic Academy of Fine Arts, gathers rare heirloom collections alongside newly sculpted master sets by contemporary women-led cooperatives headed by Kamala Devi and Sunita Pal.

Curated Miniature Collectors Pavilions

Galleries across India, boutique interior curators, and corporate collectors will have direct access to commission limited-edition heritage dioramas. Each piece comes with a provenance certificate authenticated by the West Bengal State Crafts Council.
