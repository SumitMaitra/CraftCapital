Terracotta Décor & Eco-Hospitality Sourcing Meet

A weekend networking and materials-testing meetup tailored for hospitality architects, café developers, and eco-resort interior designers.

🗓️Every Saturday & Sunday
📍Dakshinapan Shopping Complex & Biswa Bangla Haat (Rajarhat)
⏰11:00 AM – 6:00 PM

Sustainable Clay for Modern Architectural Projects

Hotels and luxury eco-resorts across Bengal, Odisha, and Northeast India are rapidly phasing out synthetic plastic planters, ceramic glazes with heavy metals, and generic import decor in favor of porous, breathable, all-natural red terracotta.

This recurring weekend meetup offers procurement managers physical samples of frost-resistant terracotta exterior planters, acoustic porous wall bricks, ambient clay lighting pendants, and food-safe unglazed tableware.

What Attendees Receive

Architects and purchasing heads receive on-the-spot material spec sheets, porosity and compressive strength testing certificates, and direct SHG pricing catalogs for orders exceeding 50 units.
