🎪 Official Trade Expo

West Bengal State Handicrafts & Terracotta Expo 2026

Eastern India's premier B2B and institutional gathering for authentic clay craft traditions, live artisan pottery demonstrations, and bulk festival sourcing.

🗓️ August 28 – September 5, 2026
📍 Biswa Bangla Mela Prangan (Milan Mela), Kolkata
⏰ 11:00 AM – 8:30 PM Daily

About the Expo

Organized in partnership with the West Bengal State Handicrafts Development Corporation, the 2026 edition of the State Terracotta Expo brings over 60 verified master sculptors and Self-Help Group (SHG) cooperatives directly from the heartlands of Panchmura (Bankura) and Ghurni (Krishnanagar).

For corporate gifting procurement officers, hotel architects, and art institutions, the expo features exclusive B2B networking pavilions with on-spot sample evaluations, custom branding consultation, and wholesale contract finalization with zero middlemen markups.

Live Demonstrations & Highlights

Witness the centuries-old traditional four-piece wheel throwing process used to sculpt the world-famous Bankura terracotta horses. Master artisan Ranjit Kumar Das and Krishnanagar clay doll specialist Kamala Devi will be conducting masterclasses on natural river silt preparation, non-toxic pigment formulation, and open wood-kiln firing techniques.

Featured Daily Schedule

12:00 PM

Live Panchmura Horse Throwing

Demonstration of modular hollow clay sculpting on traditional manual wheels.

03:30 PM

B2B Procurement & Custom Sample Desk

One-on-one sessions for corporate gifting managers, interior designers, and hotel buyers.

06:00 PM

Krishnanagar Miniature Painting Workshop

Live hand-painting session showcasing 400-year-old realistic folk doll detailing.

Institutional Procurement Support

CraftCapital representatives will be stationed at Hall 3, Desk 14, to assist enterprise buyers with consolidated GST invoicing, laboratory breakage testing reports, custom logo embossing, and Pan-India logistics coordination.
