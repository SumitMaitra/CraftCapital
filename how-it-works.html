Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

MOQs vary by product and artisan, typically ranging from 10 to 50 units for mid-sized items. Exact MOQs are listed on each product page.

How is delivery handled?

CraftCapital partners with secure logistics providers specializing in fragile goods. We coordinate pickup from the cluster and safe delivery to your specified location.

What are the payment terms?

Standard B2B terms apply: a 50% advance to initiate production, and 50% upon successful quality check before dispatch.

Do you provide GST invoices?

Yes, fully compliant GST invoices are provided for all transactions to facilitate your corporate accounting and input tax credit claims.

How does CraftCapital make money?

We charge a flat 10% commission on the final order value, which covers platform maintenance, curation, and procurement support services.

Can we request custom designs?

Absolutely. For bulk corporate gifting or specific hotel decor needs, we facilitate custom design discussions and prototyping with the artisans.

Are the artisans verified?

Yes, every artisan undergoes an on-ground verification process to ensure they are authentic practitioners from recognized clusters like Panchmura or Krishnanagar.

What happens if items arrive damaged?

We ensure premium packaging for fragile terracotta. In the rare event of transit damage, our support team assists with claims and replacements as per our B2B policy.
