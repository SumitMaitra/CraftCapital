Diwali 2026 Institutional Gifting Pre-Booking Opens Early: 15% Early-Bird Window

✍️ By Aniket Roy • 📅 August 20, 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read
Hand-sculpted terracotta diyas prepared by Bankura artisan cooperatives for corporate employee hampers.

KOLKATA — To avoid the last-minute festival procurement rush that often overwhelms traditional handicraft clusters, CraftCapital has opened its corporate Diwali 2026 pre-order booking window with direct artisan batch allocations.

Companies procuring festive employee gifts can select from curated sustainable hampers featuring hand-painted Panchmura horse miniatures, 9-piece floral ritual diya sets, and natural clay aroma diffuser sets packaged in zero-plastic biodegradable boxes.

"When corporate buyers book 6 to 8 weeks early, artisan families can plan their clay preparation and kiln firings smoothly without compromising on artistic quality or relying on exploitative moneylenders." — Subhasis Banerjee, Head of B2B Logistics

Festive Corporate Gift Box — Diwali Edition

₹1,800 / box  ·  Custom Logo Card Included

Early-bird pre-orders finalized before September 15 receive complimentary custom corporate greeting cards with artisan biographical notes and guaranteed dispatch before October 20, 2026.
