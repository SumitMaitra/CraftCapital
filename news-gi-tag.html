QR-Code Origin Authentication Rolled Out for Certified Krishnanagar Clay Dolls

✍️ By Subhashree Mukherjee • 📅 August 16, 2026 • ⏱️ 4 min read
Authentic Krishnanagar clay dolls now ship with tamper-evident GI verification certificates and scannable QR origin codes.

KRISHNANAGAR, NADIA — In a significant victory for the traditional clay artisans of Ghurni, the West Bengal State Handicrafts Council and the Department of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises have implemented an anti-counterfeit QR seal system across all institutional export shipments.

With machine-cast resin and plaster-of-paris imitations flooding retail markets under the guise of "handcrafted Bengal clay," the new digital provenance system allows institutional buyers and overseas collectors to scan the packaging seal to verify the artisan's identity, SHG cooperative registration, and geographic origin of the clay.

"This digital seal protects our craft heritage. When a buyer scans the box, they see my photograph, my village name, and the batch firing number. It proves that the piece in their hand was sculpted on the banks of the Jalangi river." — Sunita Pal, Master Artisan
