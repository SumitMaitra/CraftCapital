Intricately hand-carved terracotta wall panel featuring traditional Madhubani motifs. Fired using the indigenous Panchmura technique for a rich, natural rustic finish. Perfect for corporate gifting or boutique hotel décor.

Total: ₹21,250
ℹ️ Need procurement help? Our dedicated team will contact you within 24 hours to assist with shipping logistics and bulk customization.

This exquisite terracotta wall panel is a testament to the skill of Panchmura artisans. Blending the robust texture of Bankura clay with the delicate, narrative style of Madhubani art, this piece serves as a striking focal point for any interior space.

Each panel is entirely hand-sculpted. The artisans prepare the local clay by mixing it with sand to prevent cracking during firing. The intricate designs are carved freehand using bamboo tools before the piece is left to sun-dry for several days. Finally, it undergoes open-air firing in traditional kilns, a process that imparts the distinctive earthen terracotta red and subtle charcoal gradients.
