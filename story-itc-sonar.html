How ITC Sonar Kolkata Sourced 1,400 Terracotta Planters Directly from Panchmura

A blueprint for hospitality groups seeking sustainable, zero-plastic regional decor with transparent artisan payouts.

The Challenge

During the luxury garden and indoor courtyard renovation of ITC Sonar Kolkata, the design team sought authentic regional earthen planters that would reflect Bengal's rich cultural heritage. However, commercial retail brokers quoted inflated rates with 6 to 8 week delivery windows and could not provide material durability guarantees.

The CraftCapital Solution

Using CraftCapital’s institutional B2B procurement network, ITC Sonar bypassed 4 layers of middlemen and contracted directly with 22 artisan families in Panchmura, Bankura. CraftCapital provided consolidated quality inspection, bubble-free impact packaging, and scheduled batch dispatches.

Measurable Impact

The project generated ₹9.6 Lakh in direct bank transfers to participating potter families, representing an average 3.2x increase over what traditional middleman dealers had historically offered. Zero breakage was recorded during the 180 km transport from Bankura to Kolkata.
